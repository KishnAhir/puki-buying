4
How Long We Keep It

✅ Your data is automatically deleted 6 months after your sale is completed.

  • Active sale data is kept for the duration of the transaction
  • After the sale is completed, data is retained for 6 months for legal compliance
  • After 6 months, all personal data is permanently and irreversibly deleted
  • You can request early deletion at any time — see Section 6

5
Who We Share It With

We share your data with as few parties as possible — only those required to complete your sale:

  • Royal Mail — your name and address are used to generate your postage label only

  • No one else — we never sell, rent, or share your data with any other company, advertiser, or third party

6
Your Rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access Request a copy of all data we hold about you.
Right to Erasure Request that we permanently delete all your data.
Right to Rectification Request that we correct any inaccurate data we hold.
Right to Object Object to how we process your data at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: info@pukidevices.co.uk
We will respond within 30 days.
