FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before selling

How do I know this isn't a scam?
+
PUKI Devices is run by Kishan Ahir, a real person based in the UK. We use tracked Royal Mail and pay via bank transfer or PayPal with a clear reference. Every transaction is fully documented.
Why do you need photos of my phone?
+
Photos help us give you the most accurate offer possible. We ask for front, back, sides and IMEI screen. This means no surprises on arrival and no revised offers — what we quote is what we pay.
What if my phone doesn't match what I described?
+
If condition differs from what was described AND the photos, we will send a revised offer. You can accept or we return your phone FREE within 3 working days. We never keep phones without your agreement.
How quickly do I get paid?
+
Payment is sent the same day your phone passes inspection. Bank transfers show within 2 hours. PayPal is instant.
Do I need to factory reset my iPhone?
+
Yes! Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Erase All Content. Make sure Find My iPhone is OFF. Need help? Email us and we'll guide you step by step.
How do I find my IMEI number?
+
Dial *#06# on your iPhone. Or Settings → General → About → IMEI. It's a 15-digit number. We check this against stolen phone databases — it protects you too!
What if my phone gets lost in the post?
+
We send you a tracked Royal Mail Tracked 48 label. Every shipment is tracked end to end. In the unlikely event of loss, Royal Mail compensation covers the value.
